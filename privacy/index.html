Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

БиблиотекарьPackage: com.librarian.appsadmin@libnet.kg

This Privacy Policy explains how LibNet (“we”, “us”) handles information when authorized library staff use the Библиотекарь application (“Application”). The Application is a professional workplace for catalog digitization, MARC preparation, review, and export.

1. Who controls the data

LibNet operates the Application platform. A participating library also determines which employees may use the Application and how its institutional catalog and attendance information is managed. Depending on the context, the library may act as an independent data controller. Privacy questions can be sent to sadmin@libnet.kg.

2. Information we process

  • Account and staff profile data: name, email address, password-derived authentication data, phone number, office number, duties, job title, department, profile photo, assigned library, role, and permissions.
  • Library and catalog data: photographs of catalog cards, inventory-number fragments, selected bibliographic pages, titles, authors, identifiers, classifications, MARC records, inventory and storage-location data, processing status, corrections, exports, and related operational history.
  • Personal scanner records: MARC drafts and inventory data saved by the record owner. Source photographs in this workflow are processed for recognition but are not stored with the personal scanner record.
  • Attendance data: precise location, reported accuracy, mock-location indicator, capture time, approximate distance from the library, QR validation result where enabled, and check-in attempt outcome. Location is requested only after the user initiates a check-in; the Application does not monitor location in the background.
  • Security and technical data: account identifiers, authentication sessions, request timestamps, operational error information, IP address and other server logs reasonably required to secure and operate the service.

3. How information is used

We process information to:

  • authenticate users and enforce library roles and permissions;
  • capture, digitize, review, export, and preserve library records;
  • provide optional attendance check-in requested by the user;
  • perform OCR and AI-assisted bibliographic extraction;
  • protect the service, investigate errors, and maintain auditability;
  • respond to support, access, correction, and deletion requests.

4. Device permissions

Camera access is used to photograph catalog cards, inventory numbers, QR codes, and bibliographic pages. Photo-library access is used only when the user selects or saves an image. Precise location is used only for an attendance check-in initiated by the user. Internet access is required for authentication, protected storage, synchronization, and configured recognition services.

5. Service providers and data transfers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, protected file storage, and server functions.
  • Google ML Kit performs supported OCR operations on the device.
  • Google Gemini processes selected catalog-card or bibliographic-page content only when the relevant library has enabled and configured AI-assisted recognition. Requests pass through an authenticated server function; API keys are not embedded in the app.
  • Google Books, Open Library, Library of Congress, and Bibliothèque nationale de France may be queried by the protected ISBN service using an ISBN and source selection. User credentials are not sent to these bibliographic sources.

Providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. We use access controls and encrypted network connections appropriate to the service.

6. No advertising or sale of data

The Application does not display advertising, use advertising identifiers, sell personal information, or use personal information for cross-service behavioral advertising. It does not contain a user-facing self-registration flow; employee accounts are provisioned by an authorized administrator.

7. Storage, visibility, and retention

Catalog images and profile photos are held in private storage. Information is visible only to authenticated users whose role and library membership permit access. Data is retained while needed for library operations, record integrity, security, and applicable legal obligations. Personal scanner records can be deleted by their owner.

If an employee account has no referenced operational history, it may be deleted completely. If operational or audit records must remain connected for integrity, authentication access is removed and the profile is deactivated and anonymized: the email is replaced, and phone, office, duties, department, job title, permissions, and profile photo are removed. Necessary non-contact operational and audit history may be retained.

8. Your choices and requests

Users may update available profile details in the Application and may ask their library administrator or LibNet to access, correct, or delete personal information. To request account or data deletion, use the Data Deletion page or email sadmin@libnet.kg. We may need to verify identity and library affiliation before acting on a request.

9. Security

We apply authenticated access, role-based authorization, private storage, encrypted connections, server-side permission checks, and administrative audit records. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

The Application is a workplace tool for authorized library personnel and is not directed to children.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy when the Application or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
